# Sources

Every number this study takes from outside comes from one of the works below. "Read in full" means the whole text was in hand when the value was copied; the page, table or figure is given for each value.

## Used

1. The floor. Satte, S. (2026). The cell-to-cell lifetime floor in the Geslin et al. dynamic-cycling dataset (v1.0.1). Zenodo. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23205413. Values copied from `results/results.json` at github.com/siddharth161204-wq/geslin-floor-pilot, tag v1.0.1, commit 103f7e7 (SHA-256 of the file in `floor.json`). Mean, median and 95th percentile of the duplicate-pair difference at 90 % SOH (key `A1_pair_relative_difference.soh0.900`), worst-of-8 median shortfall (key `A3_selection_only_worst_of_N.soh0.900.worst_of_N_shortfall.8.nonparametric_median`) and their bootstrap intervals.

2. The dataset's protocol. Geslin, A., Xu, L., Ganapathi, D., Moy, K., Chueh, W. C. & Onori, S. Dynamic cycling enhances battery lifetime. Nature Energy 10, 172-180 (2025). https://doi.org/10.1038/s41560-024-01675-8. Read in full. Used: 92 commercial SiOx-graphite/NCA cells at 35 C; every cell charged CC-CV at C/2 to 4.2 V with a 0.05C cut-off; dynamic discharges at average rates C/16 to C/2 between 4.2 and 3.1 V (Methods, p. 177).

3. Law 1. Kucinskis, G., Bozorgchenani, M., Feinauer, M., Kasper, M., Wohlfahrt-Mehrens, M. & Waldmann, T. Arrhenius plots for Li-ion battery ageing as a function of temperature, C-rate, and ageing state: An experimental study. Journal of Power Sources 549, 232129 (2022). https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpowsour.2022.232129. Open access, CC BY 4.0 (licence confirmed in the Crossref record and on p. 1). Read in full from the open repository copy, https://dspace.lu.lv/items/94474306-225f-452e-a42f-96b7eec6c62d. Used: cell design (Table 1, p. 2; Section 2.1, pp. 2 to 3); test protocol and 1 kHz resistance (p. 3); cycles to 95, 90, 85 and 80 % SoH at charge rates 0.2C, 0.4C and 0.6C and ambient -10 to 55 C (Fig. 7, p. 8, printed values, transcribed in `kucinskis2022_fig7_cycles.csv`); the values and statements quoted on p. 7 for V1.

4. Law 2. Schimpe, M., von Kuepach, M. E., Naumann, M., Hesse, H. C., Smith, K. & Jossen, A. Comprehensive modeling of temperature-dependent degradation mechanisms in lithium iron phosphate batteries. Journal of The Electrochemical Society 165(2), A181-A193 (2018). https://doi.org/10.1149/2.1181714jes. Open access, CC BY 4.0 (p. A181). Read in full from the publisher's PDF. Used: Table I (p. A182), Table II and the test description (p. A182), Eqs. 9, 15, 18, 21, 23 and 24 (pp. A185 to A188), Table IV (p. A190), Eqs. A1 and A2 and Table AI (pp. A192 to A193), the statements on p. A183 and p. A190 used for V1. All in `schimpe2018_parameters.json`.

5. Proxy cell geometry and thermal properties. The two-cell study: Satte, S. (2026). Where and when a neighbour cell first reaches thermal-runaway onset temperatures: resolved against lumped, a pre-registered two-cell LG M50 pilot (v1.0.0). Zenodo. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23253738; github.com/siddharth161204-wq/m50-two-cell-pilot at tag v1.0.0. Its `inputs/oregan2022_properties.txt` (copied here unchanged) holds the effective jelly-roll properties computed from O'Regan, K., Brosa Planella, F., Widanage, W. D. & Kendrick, E. Thermal-electrochemical parameters of a high energy lithium-ion cylindrical battery. Electrochimica Acta 425, 140700 (2022), https://doi.org/10.1016/j.electacta.2022.140700, through the PyBaMM "ORegan2022" parameter set. Its solver files are copied unchanged into `src/twocell/`. The nickel strip conductance is its D24; the radiation and convection forms are its D5 and D6.

6. Capacity. Chen, C.-H., Brosa Planella, F., O'Regan, K., Gastol, D., Widanage, W. D. & Kendrick, E. Development of experimental techniques for parameterization of multi-scale lithium-ion battery models. Journal of The Electrochemical Society 167, 080534 (2020). https://doi.org/10.1149/1945-7111/ab9050. Open access, read in full. Used: "The rated capacity of the LGM50 cell between 2.5 - 4.2 V is cited to be 5 Ah" (p. 9). Checked for a full-cell DC resistance: none is reported (Table VI gives half-cell charge-transfer resistances).

7. Resistance (derived). PyBaMM 26.9.0.0 (Sulzer, V., Marquis, S. G., Timms, R., Robinson, M. & Chapman, S. J. Python Battery Mathematical Modelling (PyBaMM). Journal of Open Research Software 9, 14 (2021), https://doi.org/10.5334/jors.309), parameter set "ORegan2022" (O'Regan et al. 2022 with Chen et al. 2020), computed by `src/lgm50_resistance.py`.

8. Cold-plate base conductance (secondary arrangement). Satte, S. Current collection and event duration set the error of uniform heat-generation models of base-cooled prismatic lithium-ion cells. SSRN preprint (2026), https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.7570666; code and data https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23053419. Used: the 600 W/(m2 K) base-to-plate conductance.

9. Post hoc (DECISIONS.md D24): the dataset authors' released per-cell discharge features, `data/protocol_features.csv` (mean discharge current and normalized current variance) with `data/metadata.pkl` (cell and protocol names), at https://github.com/geslina/dynamic_cycling_Nature_Energy_2024, commit 5b2f7f04d05072fe1f9bd8af664f23eadbde6317 (SHA-256 in `results/posthoc.json`; the metadata file is the one the floor study checked). Geslin et al. describe these features as computed from one representative ageing cycle of time-series current data (Methods). The normalisation of the current variance, variance over mean in C-rate units, is established from the data by two exact identities of the two-level constant-current protocols (D24), not from the Supplementary Method that defines it, which was not read. The upstream files are not included here; the two features used appear per cell, attributed, in `results/dataset_protocol_heat.csv`. The dataset is CC BY 4.0 at the Stanford Digital Repository (doi.org/10.25740/td676xr4322); the code repository carries no licence file of its own.

## Values without a primary table

- Air conductivity 0.03 W/(m K) for the gap conduction, and the can's 15 W/(m K) in the resolved cell: inherited from the two-cell study, which gives no primary table for them. Post hoc (D24), the C/2 module spread changes by +3.7 % and -3.4 % with the air conductivity 10 % lower and higher (`results/posthoc.json`).
- Nickel 90 W/(m K) for the strip: the two-cell study's value, which gives the brief's 12 mW/K for two 0.2 by 8 mm strips; the strip sensitivity (none, one, two strips) is in `results/comparison.json`.
- The steel can's mass of about 9 g and specific heat of 500 J/(kg K), used only in the post-hoc time constants (D24): the two-cell study's estimates (`src/twocell/lumped_two_cell.py`), without a primary table.
- The 21700 format of the dataset's cells: Geslin et al.'s main text says commercial cylindrical EV energy cells and does not name the format; this study does not depend on it.
