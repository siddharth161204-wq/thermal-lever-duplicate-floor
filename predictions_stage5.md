# Predictions, Stage 5

The lever within the set: the replicate cells of a KIT parameter set or a Sandia group sat at slightly different temperatures, and Stage 5 asks whether their lifetime differences follow the between-set lever of Stages 2 and 3 times their temperature difference, by how much, and whether these data could have shown it.

Written from the archived pair files of Stages 2 and 3, their levers, and Stage 4's floors and noise model, before any Stage 5 computation: no log-lifetime ratio, predicted ratio, slope, sign count, residual or power had been computed. Known when committed: DECISIONS.md D71 to D78, including the readings fixed before this commit. The 36 KIT pairs at 0 C carry no measured lever and are not scored, so 106 KIT pairs in 36 sets are. Every 25 C set is scored in the band of the narrower interval: the warm band for all twelve KIT protocols and for the Sandia NMC and NCA cells, the cold band for LFP. The Sandia temperature difference is measure (a), and the four LFP pairs at 35 C carry no lever, so 56 Sandia pairs in 21 groups are scored, all of them tied within their check spacing. The band's median lever is Stage 4's (D57): +1.30 % per K warm, -3.58 % per K cold. p = -s dT, so that beta = 1 means the within-set lever equals the between-set one. s is in % per K, positive when the warmer cell reaches the threshold sooner; the scoring rules are D78. Each prediction is followed by its expectation, the brief's, written with it and unscored; in P1's expectation the 142 pairs and the median 0.6 K are the brief's description of the KIT replicates, 0.59 K being the median temperature range within a set.

P1. The 95 % interval of beta for KIT includes zero.
Expectation: uncertain (142 pairs at a median 0.6 K against a 7 % floor is a weak test, but not a hopeless one).

P2. The 95 % interval of beta for KIT also includes one.
Expectation: likely.

P3. The within-set warm-band lever in KIT (B2) has the sign of the band's median between-set lever (positive).
Expectation: uncertain.

P4. The within-set cold-band lever in KIT is negative.
Expectation: uncertain.

P5. The sign agreement in KIT (B3) is not distinguishable from one half (binomial p above 0.05).
Expectation: likely (Stage 2's 40 %).

P6. The smallest detectable |s| in KIT at 80 % power (C1) exceeds the band's median between-set |s| in both bands.
Expectation: likely.

P7. The Sandia pairs, tied within the check spacing in 59 of 60, give a beta interval wider than KIT's by at least a factor of two.
Expectation: likely.

P8. The pooled beta interval excludes values above 3 (a within-set lever three times the between-set one).
Expectation: likely.
