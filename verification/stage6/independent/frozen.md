## Readings made of the definitions

1. Cells and sets as D86: deletions by struct index, the five 2017-06-30 continuations removed and their lengths added to the 2017-05-12 cells at indices 0 to 4; a set is one batch and one exact policy_readable string. The five continuations' numbers of summary entries are 662, 981, 1060, 208 and 482, equal to the added lengths.
2. The swap of D87 (3) is applied to Tavg, Tmax and Tmin of channels 15 and 16 in 2017-05-12 and 2017-06-30 before every measure, the contact rule's swings included; IR is not swapped.
3. Contact rule (D88): swing = Tmax - Tmin per cycle; (a) median over cycles 91 to 100 below half that over 2 to 11; (b) median over 2 to 100 below half the largest such median among the entering cells of its set, sets of two or more only; (c) a step in Tavg between consecutive cycles within 2 to 100 above 3 K (the 2017-05-12 extract holds a zero placeholder at cycle 1, so cycle 1 is outside every rule); (d) five or more consecutive identical Tavg values within 2 to 100; (e) a Tmin within 2 to 100 below 25 C, or a missing value in the window; (f) the site's notes.
4. r_oe and r_h (D89): the uncentred correlation D89 states, of within-set deviations over all cells of sets of two or more, flagged cells included; the centred Pearson value is recorded beside.
5. Channel test (D91): within-set deviations of the registered T over cells of sets of two or more (flagged included); a pair for every channel and every two batches (all batch pairs, not only consecutive) in which the channel held such a cell; Pearson correlation; each permutation shuffles a batch's deviations among that batch's channels; p is the share of permutations with |r| at least the observed |r| (the (k + 1) / (n + 1) form is recorded beside).
6. Power (D92): the two-sided power P(|T'| > t_crit) of the noncentral t with N - J - 1 degrees of freedom, evaluated by my own quadrature over the chi-square density (scipy's noncentral t used only as a check at the root); Brent's method on |s| to 1e-12 relative. sd_c = sd(e) / sqrt(2), sd(e) recomputed from each floor's mean and 95th percentile by D61's smaller root (it reproduces the archived values exactly). The simulation tests the fixed-effects slope on 10,000 data sets of cell noise; the detectable |s| is found by bisection to 1e-6 relative, with the exact smallest crossing beside. The pairs form and shared-cell line use Stage 5's `power_t(n_pairs, |s| dT_rms, sd(e))` and sd(e) sqrt(F); the normal shape uses sd(e) = m sqrt(pi / 2), divided by sqrt(2) for the cell scale.
7. Lifetimes (D94 (2)): L is the stored cycle_life, plus the added length for a carried cell. The MATLAB label beside it: read literally ("the first cycle below 0.88 Ah") it is 1 for every 2017-05-12 cell, whose first summary entry is below 0.88 Ah (the zero placeholder); skipping that entry it equals L for every cell but 2019-01-24 channels 45 and 48 (1056 against 1166, 872 against 1089). Not used.
8. Part B (D93): fixed-effects slopes by within-set sums, checked against dummy-variable least squares. B2 uses the cells with a valid R, which are the first paper's 80 cells in 27 sets, in the pooled and the first-paper scope alike; s_w over the same cells is B1 on those cells. B3: no set has a shared highest T or a shared shortest life; the exact p is the Poisson-binomial sum over outcomes no more probable than the observed one (relative tolerance 1e-7, scipy's rule).
9. Bootstrap (D93 (6)): within each batch, its sets of two or more and its single-cell sets are separate strata, each resampled to its own size; every drawn copy of a set is its own set (its own constant in B, its own weighted entry in C). Implemented through count matrices and checked against explicit refits for the first 25 draws of every run (largest relative difference about 2e-11). C2 and C3 come from the same draws, C3 with each draw's own s_w.
10. Brackets (D93 (7)): every Part B and C quantity recomputed with the one change. Without flagged cells, the sets are re-counted and the strata re-formed. The r_oe bracket divides the temperature slopes (s_w, the pairs form, B2's temperature lever and s_w over the same cells, s_b, s_b - s_w), points and interval ends, by the r_oe of the scope; the resistance slope is not divided. The unscored R over cycles 2 to 10 changes only B2; C1 over sets of two or more only also resamples only those sets.
11. Scoring as D92 (6) and D96 (2), on the pooled values; P3 on the cells with a valid R.
